### Contract Analysis

| Contract Type | Churn Rate |
|---|---:|
| Month-to-month | 42.7% |
| One year | 11.3% |
| Two year | 2.8% |

**Key finding:** Month-to-month customers have the highest observed churn rate, while two-year contract customers have the lowest.

**Business implication:** The company should investigate retention opportunities among month-to-month customers. These findings are observational and do not establish causation.
